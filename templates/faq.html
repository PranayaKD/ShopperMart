Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about 10-minute shopping with ShopperMart

⚡ How does ShopperMart deliver in 10 minutes? expand_more

We use hyper-local micro-fulfillment centers ("dark stores") placed inside high-density neighborhoods. Our advanced warehouse technology routes pickers to pack orders in under 90 seconds, and local delivery partners deliver within a 2 km radius.

💳 What payment methods are supported? expand_more

We accept all major UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED), Debit and Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), NetBanking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

🛵 Is there a minimum order requirement? expand_more

There is zero minimum order value! You can order a single pack of milk or a whole week's groceries. Orders over ₹149 get free delivery automatically.

🔄 What if an item is damaged or missing? expand_more

We have a strict 100% satisfaction guarantee. If an item is missing or doesn't meet quality expectations, you can request an instant refund or replacement directly from the My Orders screen.

📍 Which areas in Bhadrak are currently covered? expand_more

We currently cover Sector 1 through 7, Town Hall area, Station Road, and surrounding localities in Bhadrak with active 10-minute fulfillment.
