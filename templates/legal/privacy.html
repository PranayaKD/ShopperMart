Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • ShopperMart India Private Limited

1. Introduction

ShopperMart ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding your personal data and ensuring transparent practices regarding how information is gathered, utilized, and stored across our 10-minute grocery and ecommerce platforms.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Full name, verified phone number, email address, and encrypted credentials.
  • Delivery Location: House/flat number, street address, pin code, and GPS coordinates to facilitate precision 10-minute fulfillment from local dark stores.
  • Transaction Records: Order items, invoices, payment methods (we do not store raw card numbers or UPI PINs; payments are secured via PCI-DSS compliant gateways like Razorpay).
  • Device & Analytics: IP address, device fingerprints, and anonymized browsing behavior to detect fraud and optimize performance.

3. How Your Information Is Used

We process your data exclusively to fulfill instant orders, calculate accurate delivery ETAs, communicate tracking updates, process refunds, and prevent fraudulent activity.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We strictly do not sell your personal data. We only share necessary location details with our delivery partners solely for the purpose of completing your delivery. All communications are encrypted with standard 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the full right to inspect, correct, or request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@shoppermart.com.
