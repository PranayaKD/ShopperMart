Returns & Refunds

Hassle-Free Doorstep Return Guarantee

1. No Questions Asked Return Policy

At ShopperMart, your satisfaction is guaranteed. If any grocery item arrives spoiled, incorrect, or missing, or if an electronic or lifestyle product is damaged or defective, we provide an immediate refund or replacement.

2. Return Windows by Category

Category Return Window Conditions
Fruits & Vegetables At Doorstep / 24 Hours If quality or freshness does not match standard.
Dairy, Bread & Eggs Same Day / 24 Hours Expired, leaking, or damaged seals.
Electronics & Gadgets 7 Days Replacement Manufacturing defect or physical transit damage.
Fashion & Lifestyle 7 Days Return Unused, with original tags intact.

3. Instant Refund Processing

Refunds for UPI and wallet payments are triggered within 2 to 4 hours of return verification. Credit/debit card reversals typically reflect in 2 to 5 business days depending on your issuing bank.

4. How to Request a Return

Go to My Orders, select your order, and tap "Request Help / Return" or ping our 24/7 team at support@shoppermart.com.
