Shipping & Delivery Policy

Superfast 10-Minute Fulfillment Standards

1. Instant 10-Minute Fulfillment Guarantee

ShopperMart operates localized dark stores within a 2 km delivery radius of our customers. When an order is placed, items are picked and packed in under 90 seconds, and dispatched immediately with a dedicated delivery rider.

2. Delivery Charges & Free Shipping

  • Orders above ₹149: FREE Delivery! Zero delivery fee.
  • Orders below ₹149: Flat nominal fee of ₹25 to cover rapid rider dispatch.
  • Handling Fee: ₹5 flat per order to ensure eco-friendly, tamper-evident thermal bagging.

3. Operational Hours

Our micro-fulfillment dark stores operate from 6:00 AM to 2:00 AM daily, 7 days a week. Orders placed within operational hours are delivered in 10 minutes.

4. Live GPS Tracking

Once dispatched, you can follow your order's real-time journey on the Order Manifest page, with live rider status updates from store departure to your door.
